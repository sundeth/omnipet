Version 1.0.0 October 2026
Download release
1.0 is the first full release. Four devices join the line-up — the Digivice iC, the Digimon Mini, the Pendulum Color Godzilla Edition and a conversion of Digimon World — Arena seasons open on Omninet, and Android keeps your Digimon living while the app is closed. Care follows each device's manual far more closely: sicknesses and injuries are counted apart, the death save has a time limit and a chance, evolutions can fail, and the devices that age their Digimon now do. Every release module was audited end to end, and every evolution route in them can now be reached in play.

Devices and Modules

  • New Digimon Mini. Ver.1.0 and Ver.2.0 on their own connection line, with the Count Bar charge, a 0–15 Strength meter whose bands add +4, +8 and +15 power and drain a point at a time, a battle record kept through evolution for its 30- and 100-battle evolutions, and an injured Mini that can neither battle nor train. Ver.3.0 waits for its data.
  • New Digimon Pendulum Color Godzilla Edition. G-Cells that turn Red and add power, training beside Anguirus, encounters that come round in rotation, failed evolutions and the Traited Egg they lead to, eight backgrounds earned by battling Pendulum Color devices, and jogress with Pendulum Color Digimon.
  • Improved Digital Monster Ver.Revival. Now covers the new devices' whole line-up: Ver.Revival E with the Meicoo egg and an obtainable Meicoo line, a stomach of 5 that refuses after one extra piece of meat, and no DP shown on a device that has none.
  • Renamed DMOG and PENOG. The original Digital Monster and Pendulum modules are now DMOG and PENOG, to tell them apart from the later lines. Saves carry over: pets, adventure progress, unlocks, cards and Friends move to the new names on load.
  • Fixed Module data checked against the manuals. The Digital Monster X's Filling Meat prize never dropped from its twelve enemies; the Pendulum Z's bosses now drop their items and Jumper Gate turns up as a random item; the Digital Monster Color's D-1 Grand Prix backgrounds come after 10 and 100 connection wins; the version 16 Ultimates of the Digital Monster Ver.20th and Ver.Revival have the firmware's 53 DP.
  • Fixed Sprites. Sixteen Colosseum Digimon in the Dot library each wore the drawing of the one before them — Gallantmon looked like Imperialdramon: Paladin Mode. Dinomon now uses the Pendulum Color Godzilla's official sheet, and missing frames and Specials were filled in across the libraries.
  • Fixed Digital Monster Ver.Revival evolutions match the Ver.20th's. On the Slayerdra and Breakdra eggs, Wingdramon raised with 3-4 care mistakes becomes WarGreymon (Anime) and Groundramon becomes MetalGarurumon (Anime), and the two can jogress into Omnimon again.
  • Improved Official Raptordramon sprites. Raptordramon's colour sprites are now the official ones from the Digimon Pendulum COLOR Godzilla Edition, wherever its colour sprites are used.

Digivice iC

  • New Digivice iC 10X and 20X. Reliability; DigiSoul charged from plates — real ones over NFC or the digital ones in your collection — and fed to steer evolution; Fight Money with Item, Weapon and Rare shops; DigiShops that open after connection wins; the Coliseums; accessories worn on the Digimon; the RPM charge and the money minigames (Building Smash, Poop Dodge, Shuffle, Digi Whack, Digi Mash). The iC has no DP and no Strength meter: battles and every training cost a hunger heart. The Digivice Burst follows in 1.1.
  • New Generations. An iC Digimon that dies at Perfect or later leaves its Digitama, and hatching it starts the next generation: per generation, each evolution from Child up comes an hour earlier and the Digimon starts with a point more Reliability, up to eight.
  • New Plate battles. Six Plate Charge results start a battle, and its prize — Aura, Metal Armor or Reliability Max — comes with the win.
  • New Gotcha! The 20X's Chuumon game joins the money minigames: three, five or seven catches pay 10, 30 or 50 Fight Money, and a miss shows your own Digimon's defeat.
  • New Status screen. A Next Evolution box shows the silhouette of the form the Digimon is heading for, or a ? while it meets none. The Connect Points meter fills with every battle against another device or pet and gives an item when full. Hunger's tooltip gives the stomach's fill behind the four hearts.
  • Improved Battle injuries. iC battles injure at each Digimon's own injury chance, which rises a point with every loss and falls a point with every win. The extra flat 10% roll is gone.
  • New Answering your Digimon's calls. Reliability now also rises by one when you answer an evolution call or a dying call. When your Digimon evolves on its own it calls you for a few minutes; press any button on the main screen to answer. When it is near death, the presses that try to save it answer its call too.
  • New Special Coliseum line-ups by Field. After a connection, the iC 20X's Special Coliseum sends three opponents of one Field: a Child, an Adult, then a Perfect or Ultimate. Falcomon, Peckmon, then Ravemon or Crowmon; Kamemon, Gawappamon, then JumboGamemon or Shaujinmon; Kudamon, Reppamon, then Chirinmon or Kentaurosmon. The earlier Elecmon, Bakemon, LoaderLeomon line-up can still appear. Clearing any of them still gives a Fowl Buraiga.

Digimon World

  • New Digimon World. A conversion of the 1999 PlayStation game: its Digimon, evolution requirements, care and areas adapted to Omnipet's rules, on the Digital Monster X's connection line. It has 66 collectible cards, techniques and disks as one-battle items, and Digimon that die of old age after 15 days of their own life.
  • New Recruits. Every recruit joins as a Friend and rewards something: its own card as a shiny copy, a card pack, or Giant Meat. Seadramon opens Beetle Land and Whamon's ferry Factorial Town; side areas like these are passed by and open later among the areas already cleared.
  • New Restaurant, Meat Farm and fish. Meramon opens the Restaurant and other recruits add their dishes at the game's prices. The Meat Farm is a new kind of daily quest, a gift with nothing to do, worth three Giant Meat a day. The six fish turn up as random items.
  • New Card Shop. Six different cards on offer each day, priced by rarity.

Raising Rules

  • New Sicknesses and injuries counted apart. A sick Digimon shows dots, an injured one a skull, and each kind has its own count in the form, its own untreated timer and its own limit from the device's manual: 20 and 20 on the Digital Monster X, the original Pendulum, the Pendulum Z and the Pendulum Ver.20th; 12 and 12 on the Pendulum Color, the Pendulum Color Godzilla and D-3; 30 and 30 on the iC and Mini; on the other release devices only injuries have a limit, 12 to 20 by device. Battles only injure; poop, 99G and broken sleep make a Digimon sick or injured as its device does. Heal lets you choose which to treat when the party has both, medicines cure their own kind, and D-3, the Pendulum Color and the Pendulum Color Godzilla show a Sicknesses box on the status screen.
  • New Lifespans. On the Digivice iC, a Digimon that still cannot evolve two hours after its evolution time (an Ultimate at 144 hours) goes near death; if saved, it is near death again an hour later unless it has evolved. In Digimon World a Digimon dies of old age, counted on its own clock: time in the Freezer or with the desktop game closed does not age it. On the Xros Wars Color, from five days old hunger drains and poop comes twice as fast, and 16 care mistakes in one form kill. Digimon on the other devices live on.
  • Improved Death save with a time and a chance. The Digivice iC needs 60 presses of B in 30 seconds, then survives 1 time in 20; the Pendulum Color and Pendulum Color Godzilla need 50 shakes in 30 seconds (the Pendulum Color asked for 100); the Digimon Mini and Digital Monster X need 100 presses of B, and D-3, the original Pendulum, the Pendulum Ver.20th and the Pendulum Z 100 shakes, within a minute; the other devices have no death save. The save runs on the real clock instead of to the next game minute.
  • New Starvation. A Digimon left with an empty Hunger or Strength meter dies as its manual says: after 12 hours on the Digital Monster Color, Color Godzilla 70th, Monster Hunter, Ver.20th and Ver.Revival, 6 on the original Digital Monster; any food ends the wait. On the Pendulum Color, Pendulum Color Godzilla and D-3 it dies after 20 drains find its Hunger empty, and on the Xros Wars Color after 18 drains find its Hunger, or 18 its Strength, empty; filling the meter completely resets those counts. Before, an empty meter called once and never mattered again.
  • Fixed Bedtime calls. An unanswered bedtime call costs one care mistake, as on the devices, instead of one every few minutes all night — enough to kill a Digital Monster Ver.20th, Ver.Revival or Mini Digimon left with the lights on.
  • Fixed Pendulum Color by its manual. A sick Digimon can battle (an injured one still cannot), jogress costs nothing, every battle sheds 4G and protein adds 2G.
  • Improved Vital Values. The Child ceiling is now 4000 — the BE's own routes asked for more than the old 2500 — Vital Values carry over from Baby I to Child, and Adventure battles pay the BE's table, so every Vital Bracelet route can be reached by raising without care mistakes.

Evolution

  • New Failed evolutions. A Digimon whose evolution routes read its win ratio gets one try when its evolution time comes: if it meets no route, or loses the chance roll, the evolution fails and the timer never evolves it again. Items, jogress, armor evolution and Level Reverse still can. On the Pendulum Color, Pendulum Color Godzilla, Pendulum Ver.20th, Digital Monster X and Pendulum Z a failed Digimon's hearts then deplete twice as fast and it poops twice as often, as an Ultimate does after 48 hours in its form.
  • Fixed Every route can be reached. Every evolution route in the release modules was played through by an automated check, and the ones that could never happen now can. The Pendulum Ver.20th's Nightmare Soldiers, Wind Guardians, Metal Empire and Virus Busters Digitama never unlocked, leaving 115 Digimon out of reach; several jogress routes named partners that could not answer (Vital Bracelet, BE, Pendulum Z); the Digital Monster X's routes now follow the guide; and the Pendulum Ver.20th's Jesmon (X Antibody) comes from a Jesmon that beats an Alphamon in a connection battle.
  • New Jogress across modules and over the cable. Two Digimon from modules on the same connection line can jogress — the Pendulum Color with the Pendulum Color Godzilla, the Digital Monster Color line, the Digital Monster Ver.20th with the Ver.Revival, Digital Monster X with Digimon World — each evolving by its own module's route. A jogress route's own level and area requirements now count, so the Pendulum Z's Level 6 jogress can happen, and a real Pendulum Color or Pendulum Z can jogress with your Digimon through the DCom adapter.
  • New Evolution items. Items that evolve a Digimon — D-3's DigiMentals, the iC's evolution items, Digimon World's Black Wing — are used from the Armor / Item menu, each use counting toward its route.
  • New DigiXros and Mode Change in the Digidex. Every temporary evolution registers in the Digidex and earns its Album entry, so the Xros Wars Color's Album Digimon — Shoutmon (King Version), Gumdramon, OmniShoutmon and ZeigGreymon — can now be obtained.
  • Fixed Area progress. An evolution that asks for an area cleared on the device, like the Digital Monster X's, counts once any of your Digimon has cleared it, and Digimon that cannot battle (the Color Godzilla 70th's Koromon, the BE's babies) use the area cleared before them. Adventure unlocks follow the version of the Digimon that won.
  • New DigiXros costs DP (Xros Wars Color). Each DigiXros now costs DP, as on the device: 1 DP for Shoutmon X2 up to 7 DP for Shoutmon X7 (Superior Mode included). The battle form picker shows each form's cost and only offers forms your Digimon has the DP for; taking one spends the DP, and an adventure form lasts the whole World.
  • Fixed Arresterdramon's Superior Modes follow the World. On the Xros Wars Color, Arresterdramon's Superior Mode is offered everywhere except World 20, and its Brave Snatcher mode only in World 20, as on the device.

Battle

  • Improved Connection battles count. Every battle against another player or device — Versus, DCom with a real device and online PvP — counts in the battle and win records evolution reads, as on the devices: the iC 10X's 20 and 50 wins, the Mini's 30 and 100 battles, and the original Digital Monster's 15 battles, which the real device could only fight by connecting.
  • Improved Injuries after every battle. Versus, DCom and online PvP battles can injure as adventure battles do, at the Digimon's own injury chance; no battle makes a Digimon sick. Arena battles never injure.
  • New Battle items in battle. The iC's weapons, the X's chips and Digimon World's disks are no longer used from the inventory: the adventure battle offers the module's own from a list before it starts, lasting the area. On the iC they are offered before every round and last the round, and over DCom its weapons are offered before the charge and used up by that battle.
  • New Specials. The Battle menu's Specials lists what each module in your party offers: its shops, its passwords (the Pendulum Ver.20th's Copymon) and the iC's minigames.
  • New Connection rewards that count. A reward can ask for a number of connection battles, a number of different devices, wins only, or a given device line: the Pendulum Color Godzilla's eight backgrounds (3 to 20 battles with Pendulum Color devices), the Color Godzilla 70th's five, the Digital Monster Color's File Island (four different devices) and the iC's DigiShops.
  • Fixed Versus and DCom. Versus fought other Digimon than the two you tapped when part of the party was selected, prepared Pendulum Color, Xros Wars Color and Pendulum Z Digimon for another line, and needed the Digital Monster Color module installed. A press in the half second after a DCom charge cancelled the battle.
  • Fixed The Digital Monster's version over DCom. The original Digital Monster numbers its versions from 0 when it battles: Ver.1 sends 0, Ver.5 sends 4. Omnipet sent Ver.1 as 1. Saved Digimon are corrected when they load. Thanks to the Digimon Database for the report.
  • Fixed Online battles through Discord. After linking your Discord account, one player hosts a room and the other joins it from the room list; both press START and the battle is fought like a WiFi battle, with both players' names on the versus screen. The rooms used to stop at the confirmation screen, which also showed an empty team.
  • Fixed WiFi battles. Two Omnipets on the same network can battle again. Joining never searched the network, so no host was ever found, and the confirmation screen could not start a battle. Now you choose your Digimon, one game hosts and shows its code and address, the other lists the games that answer, and the battle starts when both players press START. It is fought with the Arena's rules and counts like any other connection battle. The WiFi button needs a local network, not an internet connection. If a game on another computer is not found, allow Omnipet through that computer's firewall.
  • Fixed Battle results. A win that spent the last DP or Hunger recorded nothing and played the failure sound, and the between-round Food menu vanished after one feed.
  • Fixed Skipping the result screen lost the win. Pressing B on a battle's result screen left the battle before the round or area won, its unlocks, the Vital Bracelet drop and a connection battle's PvP count were recorded. B now only ends the wait.
  • Fixed Boss fights, losses and DCom waits. A lost boss fight no longer shows WIN, every lost battle shows LOSE, and B during a boss's CLEAR keeps the win. Replaying an older area no longer changes your progress in the furthest area, and the result's Exp includes the all-alive bonus. The DCom waiting screen keeps its instructions beside the countdown, and START disappears once the wait is over.

Arena and Omninet

  • New Arena seasons. Register a team of one to three Digimon for the season (a week by default) and battle other players' teams on the server: 10 attacks a day, opponents picked fairly (whoever you have met least comes first), +25 for a win, −12 for a loss and +5 for a draw. Defences are fought for you while you are away. At the close the top three teams win 50, 25 and 10 coins, and each Digimon's record gains its team's battles.
  • New Arena hub. Ranks, rules, history and results, with a replay of every battle from the history. The team waits in the Freezer until the season ends and never leaves your save. A Digimon fights one season per form — evolve it to bring it back — and only modules published on Omninet can take part.
  • Improved Omninet shop. Items can be bought again whenever you have the coins; modules, cosmetics and other unlocks stay one-time purchases. Fixed: a purchase the server refused was taken as bought, bought items and downloaded backgrounds vanished after a restart, a selected shop background was never drawn, and cosmetics were shared by every account on the device.
  • Fixed Accounts and downloads. A single server error at automatic login logged you out; a reward already credited was sent again forever; raw server errors were shown; a failed module update deleted the installed module (updates now replace it only once complete); a second tap during a download started a second install. Logging out now removes the device from your account.
  • Fixed Arena without an Omninet login. The Arena button opened the Discord link dialog when you were not signed in to Omninet; it now opens the Omninet sign-in.
  • Fixed Online messages. The Arena says "Cannot reach Omninet" instead of "No active season" when the server is down, BACK from the Discord pet selection returns to the main menu, and on Android the Discord link is kept in the app's storage (a link made before may have to be made again).

Training

  • Fixed Training fixes. Dummy, Punch and Count Match Classic waited forever after a charge too weak to fire; Excite paid a Good stop as Great; Count Match Classic took the trophy away from shaking past 14 and now grades 0–40 in training as in battle; Head to Head never showed the currency screen; selecting Pets in Need crashed the training menu.
  • Improved M.O.G.E.R.A. Clearing the Anti-G phase earns Strength whether or not M.O.G.E.R.A. falls: a surviving M.O.G.E.R.A. is a Good that still counts as a loss, destroying it an Excellent, and leaving during the Phase 2 charge pays nothing. Its art failed to load on the Pi, Batocera and Android.
  • Fixed Trophies. Trophies, from training and quests alike, go only to Digimon whose device shows them.
  • Fixed Training results and exits. The result banner always shows the grade the training pays (Dummy, Count Match Color and Classic, M.O.G.E.R.A.). B and EXIT leave Count Match Color, Z and Classic, Count Bar and RPM during the charge. In Head to Head, UP shoots along the upper lane.

Collection, Shops and Items

  • Improved Collection. Rebuilt on the game's standard pages and buttons. A card you have not obtained shows its number and back but never its name, every tile has a name strip that scrolls long names, and FILTER narrows the view to one module. A card is read for one Digimon of its module, chosen when there are several, and its cooldown starts only once it is read.
  • New Card pack reveal. Opening a pack shakes and bursts it, then turns each card over; a shiny one glows with sparkles. A pack bought in a shop opens at once, one won or unlocked waits for the main screen. A or a tap shows the next card, B skips.
  • New Holo cards. Shiny cards have a holographic finish whose colours sweep across the artwork.
  • Improved Module items. A module's own items go to one Digimon of that module — the inventory asks which when several could take it — while the core foods and shop items keep using the selection.
  • New Vital Bracelet battle drops. The Vital Bracelet, BE and Vital Hero never had a shop: their companion apps gave items for events and adventures. Every adventure or connection battle a Vital Bracelet Digimon wins has a 44% chance to drop an item: HP+, ATK+ and POWER+ (and their temporary versions), Vital Drinks or Battle Clocks, the smaller ones more common. The BE's own NFC battle items are unchanged. Vital Bracelet items no longer add weight, and the original Vital Bracelet's item limits were 0, so its quest rewards were always refused; it now accepts them.
  • New BE random battles. The Vital Bracelet BE's NFC battle comes as a random event against a character of the Digimon's own card, paying Vital Values by stage and its items in turn.
  • Fixed Shop purchases that did not work. Bought backgrounds drew the splash screen instead of the background; on Windows some modules failed to install with "Error, try again"; Battle Clocks and NEXT Clocks bought in the Shop showed three times over and could only be given to Vital Bracelet Digimon; bought items had no icon; after the free first module the others still read Free while the server charged 50 coins. All fixed, and every view now shows the balance the server reports after a purchase.
  • New Contribution points and Specials. Contributing to the Digimon Database with your Omnipet account's email earns contribution points: 1 per contribution, 2 when the entry is marked as needing data. They are a balance of their own, apart from coins, shown with a blue diamond. Spend them in Shop > Specials, which opens with 15 premium backgrounds at 5 points each, with day, dusk and night pictures and HD versions; once bought they are chosen like any other background.
  • Technical No starter Coins from the tutorial. Finishing the tutorial no longer grants 50 Coins: your first module from the Shop is free instead.

Android

  • New Progress with the app closed. The background service is gone. When you come back, every minute you were away is played through exactly as if the app had stayed open — care calls, poop, sickness, evolution, death, sleep and midnight — with no limit on how long you were gone.
  • New Notifications without a service. Care calls, cleanup and events are planned up to seven days ahead and delivered by Android itself, on three channels you can switch off separately, with no wakelock or battery exemption. Tapping an event's notification within five minutes opens the event. Daylight saving, a time-zone change, a clock set back or forward and a reboot are all handled.

  • Fixed Status and main screen. Stage VIII Digimon (Jesmon GX, Ogudomon X Antibody) no longer crash Status or the Digidex. The Evolution Timer shows the real time left, the Attribute tooltip reads Data (not Dark), Next Evolution and Reliability have their tooltips, trophy counts above 999 show 999, and the mouse wheel moves the pet row. iC and Digimon World Digimon show only Hunger hearts, and the first press on the screensaver only wakes the screen.
  • Fixed Start-up, setup and tutorial. RANDOM egg picks only modules you own and never the Tutorial; the message is right when no module has an egg; NO MODULE DETECTED can be retried with A; the error screen's Retry and Free Mode switch work. Setup lets Esc/B be mapped as B, and cancelling the graphics test keeps your frame rate. The tutorial's touch SKIP restores your party, and the Healing strip shows every Digimon a dose reaches.

Version 1.0.0 Beta 3 August 6, 2026
Download release
Beta 3 is about accuracy. The battle protocols were rebuilt on documented constants and verified against real hardware, the device rules that used to be hardcoded per family are now module data, and a module can finally describe the physical device a pet was hatched on separately from its evolution line. Training, jogress, Traited Eggs and power bonuses all follow the manuals now instead of one approximation per ruleset. Android's pause and resume were rewritten from the ground up.

Battle

  • Improved Battle protocol rework. Every connection protocol now reads its constants and packet layouts from a single documented source that matches the values tested against real devices. The old JSON protocol layer was removed: its field tables were never used to build packets, one file was malformed, another failed a case-sensitive lookup on Linux and Android, and a third contradicted the tested value. Where the manuals and the hardware disagree, the difference is recorded rather than silently patched.
  • Fixed DCom battles on PEN_X and COLOR. Both crashed at packet generation before ever starting a fight.
  • New Protocol test harness. A headless test suite round-trips every protocol's packets through the same validators and parsers the DCom path uses, runs all versus protocols, and checks the adventure simulator's invariants — over 500 checks.
  • Fixed Charge strength mapping. The strength sent into a battle is now decided by the module's chosen minigame instead of its ruleset. Pendulum Z runs a Count Match on the X ruleset and was being read as an XAI bar, sending a raw meter value where the minigame produces super hits. Every minigame now maps onto the four levels the protocols actually carry, including a proper "failed charge" at the bottom.
  • Improved XAI bar. Reworked to sprite art picked from the pet's attribute and level, at pixel-perfect integer scaling, with the arrow riding above the bar, recoloring to the color it landed on and holding half a second so the result is readable. Its speed is now expressed as how long a pass takes rather than a pixel rate, so it plays the same at every resolution — the easy end used to take about five seconds a sweep and felt stalled. A missed bar no longer counts as a Good.
  • Improved Attack projectiles. Shots accelerate as they fly instead of drifting at a constant speed, on a gentler curve with flat ends — no crawl at launch, no visible whip as the shot lands.
  • Fixed Mid-run eligibility. A pet that falls sick or reaches its bedtime partway through an area now stays in the run instead of silently vanishing from the layout and the minigames while still being on the team. Only running out of the battle cost drops it. The retire check logs exactly why each pet was pulled.
  • Fixed Battle costs in connection battles. Versus now hides pets that cannot fight, and DCom battles charge the module's battle cost — its result handler had copied the PvP loop without the deduction.
  • Fixed Defeat pose. A pet that loses now shows its defeat animation whether or not the fight was against a boss.
  • New Area locks. An unlock can now keep an adventure area shut until it is earned, scoped to a single version — for the devices that gate their last area behind a connection battle. Locked areas stay out of the picker and progress never moves into them.
  • New Attribute advantage as power. Modules can choose whether the attribute advantage is added to the hit rate or folded into Power before the hit rate is worked out, which is how the Digital Monster X describes it.

  • Fixed Ready sprite per minigame. Count Match Color and Count Match Z draw their own ready phase (the attribute sprite, and the arrows to match); every other minigame uses the animated ready sprite. Which is which is now declared by the minigame itself.

Devices and Eggs

  • New Physical device selection. A module can now describe the real devices it covers, and hatching walks through them first: each device is shown as its own artwork with its screen background composed into it, and picking one narrows the egg list to that device's eggs. A device with a single egg hatches it directly. Modules without device data keep the old flow, and random hatching picks a device too.
  • New Device version. A pet now carries the hardware revision it was hatched on separately from its gameplay version, and it survives every evolution. Connection battles, the OEM packets and connection unlocks all key off the hardware value; evolutions and unlocks keep using the gameplay one. Existing saves fall back to their game version.
  • New Device-pair unlocks. A versus unlock can name a device and its opponent, so "battle an XA with an XB" is one record that works in either direction, over both versus and DCom. A special opponent value means "any device other than this one", so a device can say "connect with any other version" without listing every pair.
  • Fixed Password pets. A password can state which hardware its pet counts as — a Copymon is a doll of a Digimon from another device, so it should not inherit the host module's version for protocol purposes.

Raising Rules

  • New Traited Egg and power bonus are module data. These were one hardcoded branch per device family, which stopped being able to describe the devices — the four Pendulums alone want four different power models. They are now two independent module settings: how a Traited Egg is earned (never, a stage-gated roll, a win ratio, an evolution timer, or outliving the lifespan) and where power above the species base comes from (a stage table, stage table plus Shaken, strength and level, strength hearts, effort, or stars). Every table is keyed on a full Strength meter, as the manuals say, and pays the Traited Egg column independently. Modules written before these fields migrate automatically.
  • New Training payout tables. What one training pays in effort, strength and weight is now a four-slot table indexed by the outcome — Bad, Good, Great, Excellent — the same four levels the connection protocols carry. A device that pays nothing for a failure simply starts its list at zero. All eight training modes were re-graded to report a real failure level, following each device's own bands; the Pendulum Color count match uses its manual's grading, and Mogera pays nothing when the Anti-G stage is failed. A negative strength value fills the meter outright, which is what a Megahit does on the Pendulum Ver.20th.
  • New Jogress cost. A module states which stat both pets must have at maximum to fuse — nothing, DP, Effort, Strength or Hunger — and it is consumed by the fusion. The hunger and strength requirement mirrors the hearts the status screen shows, so what the player sees is what is asked for.
  • New Evolution chance and area. Routes can now carry a percentage chance, rolled only after every other requirement passes and falling through to the next route on a failure — which is how a device presents a coin flip between two outcomes. Routes can also require a minimum adventure area cleared.
  • New Unlock by stage. An evolution unlock can name a stage rather than a specific target, for the devices that hand out a Digitama simply for raising a Digimon that far.
  • Fixed Digidex unlocks. Unlocks that count how many Digimon you have raised are now granted as soon as you evolve into the one that completes them. The Digidex screen used to be the only place they were checked, so you had to open it before the reward registered.

  • Fixed HP by level. The level table now grants +2 HP at levels 2, 5, 6, 8 and 10, matching the Digital Monster X manual; level 8 was missing.

Android

Available Downloads

  • WindowsOmnipet_Windows_Ver_0.9.8.zip — Standard Windows distribution with all modules and the Module Editor.
  • ExperimentalOmnipet_Nuitka_Windows_Ver_0.9.8.zip — Nuitka-compiled Windows build. Faster but experimental; use if the standard build has performance issues.
  • PythonOmnipet_Python_Desktop_Ver_0.9.8.zip — Cross-platform Python build for Linux and macOS. Requires pip install pygame psutil.
  • BatoceraOmnipet_Batocera_Ver_0.9.8.zip — Place in /userdata/roms/pygame/. Input configuration may need adjustment.
  • GamePiOmnipet_GamePi_Ver_0.9.8.zip — For the Raspberry Pi Zero 2W. May be installed over an existing installation in home/Games/Omnipet, or used with the pre-made image for clean installs.
Installation notes. The Module Editor source is available on GitHub only; it is not included in the Batocera and GamePi releases due to Linux incompatibility. For clean GamePi installs, the pre-made image is recommended and WiFi can be configured via the Raspberry Pi Imager. The Python Desktop build requires pip install pygame psutil. There is no automatic update system in this release.

Major Features

  • New PvP battles between devices. Two Omnipet devices may now battle over WiFi. Both devices must have compatible modules installed.
  • New Daily quests. Each day the game selects three quests for the player. Quests are module-specific and are authored in the Module Editor.
  • New Random events. Events triggered by XAI rolls can drop items or initiate special encounters. Events are configurable in the Module Editor.
  • New Library menu. A new menu option tracks and claims quest rewards. The Digidex and Freezer Box have been moved here from the Settings menu.

Debug Tools

  • Improved Debug menu. Pressing F12 on the main screen now opens a multi-page debug menu exposing all pet attributes for testing and module development.
  • New Evolution path testing. The debug interface allows module developers to test evolution paths and adjust attributes at runtime.

Module Editor

  • New Random Events tab. Authors may define custom random events with item drops and special encounters.
  • New Daily Quests tab. Authors may define daily quests with custom objectives and rewards.
  • Improved Multiple eggs. No limits on the number of eggs or modules. A single version may be used for interconnected evolution lines, or separate versions for independent lines.

Hardware and Platform Support

  • Improved GamePi Zero 2W image. A complete plug-and-play image is available. The game loads as a service on startup.
  • Improved Input mapping. Custom controls can be configured via input_config.json. An input test application is included for mapping keyboard or joystick controls; a default joystick mapping is provided.
  • Technical Raspberry Pi Imager. WiFi may be configured when writing the GamePi image using the imager's advanced options.

Game Balance

  • Improved Battle damage. Attack damage now accounts for pet level and mini-game score, based on DMX attack patterns.
  • Improved Sickness probability. Sickness chances were rebalanced: a flat rate for wins, scaled rates for losses, with protein overdose and disturbance penalties capped at 50%.
  • Note Stage 1 lifespan. Stage 1 pets are intentionally short-lived (10 minutes) and do not sleep.

Bug Fixes

  • Fixed Egg selection scene. Various issues with the egg selection scene at different resolutions and with Digidex type/egg unlock functionality were resolved.
  • Fixed Battle failure handling. Battle failure and result processing have been corrected.
  • Improved Empty module handling. Empty module folders no longer cause crashes during loading.
Platform compatibility. Windows, Linux, and GamePi are fully supported. Batocera support is maintained. GarlicOS has limited pygame support. Knulli compatibility is under investigation.

Version 0.9.4 — Modularity and Tooling June 18, 2025

Major Features

  • NewDMX module completion. All unlockables are available and all items are functional, with custom icons and effects. Only special encounters remain to be implemented.
  • NewCustom module items. Modules may now define custom items with status_boost and status_change effects. The framework is extensible to additional effect types.
  • NewStandalone Windows build. A vpet.exe file allows running the game without a Python installation. This is an initial release; bugs are expected.
  • NewDynamic resolution and frame rate. Resolution and frame rate are configurable via config.json. The game supports 4K at 144 FPS on high-end systems.
  • NewNon-Pi Linux support. Compatibility checks have been added for standard Linux distributions beyond Raspberry Pi systems.

System Improvements

  • ImprovedScene optimization. All scenes have been optimized for low-power, low-CPU devices.
  • ImprovedPet modularity. Pets are now fully modular, with all options and checks configured in module JSON files. DMC pets may now have condition hearts.
  • ImprovedModular unlockables. Modules may define their own secret evolution lines, eggs, and backgrounds.
  • ImprovedBattle menu. Now displays proper icons for module adventure modes. Up/down keys can be used for area and round selection.
  • ImprovedHigh-resolution backgrounds. Enhanced backgrounds for resolutions above 1024×768, although some module backgrounds are still pending.

Version 0.9.4b Hotfix

  • FixedEgg hatch crash. A critical crash following egg hatching has been resolved.
  • NewMulti-pet support. The pet limit is configurable in config.json. Pet size adjusts relative to resolution and pet count for optimal display.

Development Tools

  • NewModule Editor progress. The visual module creation tool is approximately 70% complete. It covers module editing, backgrounds, items, pets, evolutions, sprites, and enemies.
  • NewD-3 module development. A custom D-3 module is in development, featuring Digimentals and Armor Digivolutions.

Platform and Technical

  • TechnicalPython 3.10.7 + pygame 2.6.1. Updated to the latest stable versions. Pygame 2+ is required due to an input bug in pygame 1 causing 100% CPU usage.
  • TechnicalGamePi13 support. Full support for the Waveshare GamePi13 with pre-installed Bookworm images. An installation script is included.
  • TechnicalBatocera pygame format. Experimental support for .pygame files. Rename vpet.py to main.py, zip the project, and change the extension to .pygame.
  • TechnicalLuckFox Pico. Successful launch confirmed on the LuckFox Pico via X11 forwarding.

Community

  • CommunityHardware builds. Community collaboration on Pi Zero builds with battery and accelerometer integration. Alternative "shake" key support for devices without an accelerometer.
  • CommunityInstallation troubleshooting. Community solutions for Bullseye vs. Bookworm compatibility issues, GamePi13 setup, and permissions.
  • CommunityFuture platforms. Community interest in smartwatch ports, micro handheld compatibility, and Android.
  • CommunityBattle feedback. Community feedback on battle pacing and reward indication informed the planned battle system redesign.
